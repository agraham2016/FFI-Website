## 1. Purpose

To ensure FFI's services fully comply with the CMS Home and Community Based Services (HCBS) Final
Rule (Medicaid HCBS Final Regulation; AHCCCS HCBS Rules), whose purpose is to ensure individuals
receiving HCBS are integrated into their communities and have full access to the benefits of
community living, receiving services in the most integrated setting.

## 2. Policy

FFI applies the guiding principles of the ALTCS program — member-centered case management,
person-centered planning that safeguards against unjustified restrictions, and stakeholder
collaboration — across all services. For every member, FFI ensures:

1. **Community integration.** Services support full access to the greater community: community
   activities, competitive integrated employment (the explicit aim of ISE/GSE/PTE/ESA), and control
   of personal resources.
2. **Choice.** Members choose their services, providers, activities, schedules, and (in supported
   living) where and with whom they live, from among setting options including non-disability
   settings.
3. **Rights.** Members' rights of privacy, dignity, respect, and freedom from coercion and
   restraint are protected in every setting; FFI's prohibited-techniques rules (FFI-03) apply
   everywhere.
4. **Autonomy.** Services optimize individual initiative, autonomy, and independence in making
   life choices, including daily activities, physical environment, and with whom to interact.
5. **No unjustified restrictions.** Any modification of these conditions must be supported by a
   specific assessed need, justified and documented in the person-centered Planning Document with
   informed consent, tried-and-failed less intrusive alternatives, data collection, and review
   dates — never for staff convenience.
6. **Supported living settings (HAI/HID).** Members' homes are the member's own; members control
   their schedules, have access to food at any time, may have visitors of their choosing at any
   time (see FFI-22), and hold the same responsibilities and protections from eviction as any
   tenant under Arizona landlord-tenant law.

## 3. Procedures

- Person-centered planning participation per FFI-11; FFI never restricts a member except through
  the documented Planning Document process above.
- Staff raise potential rights restrictions to the Program Manager, who brings them to the
  member's planning team before any restriction is implemented.
- Annual self-assessment: the Quality & Compliance Coordinator completes an HCBS settings
  self-assessment for each service line annually and remediates any gaps by CAP.

## 4. Training

HCBS Rules training (community integration, member rights, person-centered practice, prohibition
on unjustified restrictions) is included in onboarding and annual refreshers, delivered by the
Training Coordinator; competency is documented.

## 5. Monitoring, compliance, and corrective action

The Quality & Compliance Coordinator monitors HCBS compliance through the annual self-assessment,
site visits, planning document reviews, and member/family feedback. Violations (unjustified
restriction, rights interference) are corrected immediately, reported where required, and
addressed through retraining and discipline.

## 6. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI HCBS settings self-assessment; Planning Document restriction-justification record.

**References:** Medicaid HCBS Final Regulation (42 CFR 441.301(c)(4)-(5)); AHCCCS Home and
Community-Based Services rules; DDD Provider Policy Manual Ch. 61; A.R.S. § 36-551.01.
