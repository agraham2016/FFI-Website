## 1. Purpose

To set standards for respectful, lawful, and responsive communication with members and their
responsible parties, per the QVA, DDD Provider Policy Manual Chapter 2, Operations Policy Manual
Chapter 1001A, A.A.C. R6-6-108.C & H, R6-6-804.8, R6-6-805.B, R6-6-806.K, R6-6-808.G & I,
R6-6-809.G & H, and A.R.S. § 36-551.01.P & Q.

## 2. Policy

- **Professional standards.** All staff communicate with members and responsible parties
  professionally and courteously — respectful tone, plain language, honoring the member's dignity,
  and timely responses (voicemail/email returned within one business day).
- **HIPAA compliance.** Information about a member is shared with family members or others **only**
  with a release of information signed by the member/guardian (FFI-01), except where law requires
  or permits disclosure. Staff verify guardianship/representative status before sharing.
- **Preferred methods.** At intake, FFI determines and documents each member's guardian/family
  preferred communication methods and frequency (phone, email, text, written progress reports,
  meetings) and honors them. Preferences are reconfirmed at least annually and at each planning
  meeting.
- **Emergency notification.** In an emergency involving the member, the responsible party is
  notified as soon as the member is safe — the same day for serious incidents, and within the
  timeframes in FFI-02 for all incident types. After-hours notifications are made by the On-Call
  Supervisor.

## 3. Procedures

1. Intake packet includes the Communication Preferences form; preferences are entered into the
   member record and visible to assigned staff.
2. Routine communications (schedule changes, progress updates) come from the assigned supervisor;
   DSPs refer questions beyond their role to the supervisor.
3. All substantive communications with responsible parties are documented in the member record
   (date, method, topic, outcome).
4. Interpretation/translation is provided at no cost when needed (FFI-13).

## 4. Training, monitoring, and oversight

Professional communication and this policy are covered at onboarding and annually. Supervisors
monitor through documentation review and family feedback (FFI-14); complaints about communication
are handled per FFI-06 §3.

## 5. Compliance and corrective action

Discourteous or unauthorized communication results in coaching, retraining, and progressive
discipline; unauthorized PHI disclosure is handled under FFI-01.

## 6. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI Communication Preferences form; contact log; Release of Information (FFI-01).
