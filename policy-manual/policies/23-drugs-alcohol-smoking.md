## 1. Purpose

To balance members' adult rights regarding alcohol and tobacco with health, safety, and a
smoke-free environment, and to set absolute rules for staff, per DDD Operations Policy Manual
Chapters 1001A and 1001B, A.A.C. R6-6-809.E & F, R6-6-808.G–I, A.R.S. § 36-568.02, and the
Federal Controlled Substances Act.

## 2. Member rights and parameters

- **Rights.** Adult members retain the right to make lawful choices about alcohol and tobacco.
  Restrictions may exist only through the member's Planning Document, based on documented health
  or safety needs (for example, medication interactions), consistent with Article 9 — this policy
  does not itself restrict member rights.
- **Documented permissions and parameters.** Any permissions and parameters for a member's use of
  alcohol/tobacco — in the community, the supported-living setting, or at day/working programs —
  are identified and documented in the member's planning documents, and staff follow them. Staff
  document observed use only as those plans require.
- **Storage and consumption.** In shared supported-living homes, member-owned alcohol is stored in
  the member's private space (or as house agreements set out); consumption respects housemates'
  rights. Staff-owned alcohol is never brought to, stored at, or consumed at any service setting.

## 3. Smoke-free environment

- Every member has the **right to live in a smoke-free environment**. Indoor smoking is not
  permitted at FFI service settings.
- Members and staff who use tobacco do so outdoors in designated areas, positioned so smoke does
  not enter living areas or affect other members. Staff smoke only on breaks, away from members.

## 4. Staff prohibitions

- Staff are prohibited from using or being impaired by alcohol or drugs **while on duty**;
  reporting to work impaired is grounds for immediate removal from duty and discipline.
- Staff never purchase or furnish alcohol, tobacco, or any controlled substance for a member
  outside documented plan parameters, and never use member funds for such purchases outside the
  member's documented, lawful choices.

## 5. Marijuana and controlled substances

Marijuana (including CBD derivatives classified as controlled) remains a Class 1 controlled
substance under federal law; see FFI-24 (Medical Marijuana) for member medical-use handling.
Illegal drug possession or use at any service setting is reported to the Executive Director and,
as applicable, law enforcement and the Division.

## 6. Rights review

This policy has been reviewed for Article 9 compliance: it imposes no restriction on member rights
beyond documented, plan-based parameters, and prioritizes the equal right of all members to a
smoke-free home.

## 7. Training, monitoring, compliance, and corrective action

Covered in onboarding and annual refreshers. Supervisors monitor during site visits. Violations
are addressed through a dual system: **disciplinary action** for the individual (up to
termination, plus incident reporting where a member was affected) and **system-based corrective
action** (procedure or environment changes) to prevent recurrence.
