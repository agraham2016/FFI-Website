## 1. Purpose

To govern development, submission, and maintenance of Daily Staffing Schedules for FFI's
supported-living (IDLA/HID) settings, per DDD Provider Policy Manual Chapter 52.

## 2. Policy

1. FFI maintains staffing ratios determined by the **collective needs of all members at the
   site**.
2. FFI submits all Staffing Schedules to the Division for review and approval **5 days before**
   members move into an Expansion Home or IDLA (HID) setting or before all known/planned events,
   and **within 2 days after** unplanned events.
3. FFI submits a new schedule for changes in occupancy, capacity (with Network approval), address,
   behavior or medication status that results in a modification to the staffing range, or home
   closure.
4. FFI submits a new schedule for school/holiday breaks or an inability of members to attend a day
   or work program that will result in changes to the staffing range.
5. **Emergencies:** FFI staffs the home as appropriate for the immediate circumstance.
6. If an emergency changes the staffing range, FFI notifies the **Network Manager the next
   business day** and the **Support Coordinator as soon as possible**, but no later than the next
   business day.
7. Staffing schedules include: members' first and last names; identification of members who have
   Behavior Plans; each member's work/day program schedule; additional staffing support needs;
   specific details regarding members' staffing needs; and, when a change is requested, the reason
   for the schedule change.
8. FFI submits an IDLA staffing schedule **annually** for approval. When a temporary staffing
   schedule is used, a new staffing schedule is submitted when the temporary schedule ends.
9. FFI meets with Network to review daily habilitation staffing schedules annually (as applicable
   to group-home settings; FFI applies the same review to IDLA settings on request).
10. FFI submits all agreed-upon updates to Staffing Schedules to DDD **within 14 calendar days**
    following the annual review.

## 3. Procedures

The Program Manager owns schedule creation using the IDLA Planning Guide (DDD-1856A) and IDLA
staffing schedule format, validates it against members' Planning Documents, and tracks submission
and approval dates. Approved schedules are posted for staff and kept in the site records.

## 4. Monitoring, compliance, and corrective action

The Quality & Compliance Coordinator verifies quarterly that operating schedules match approved
schedules and that all triggering events generated timely submissions. Deviations are corrected by
immediate resubmission and CAP.

## 5. Forms and references

**Forms:** IDLA Planning Guide (DDD-1856A); IDLA Staffing Schedule; IDLA Assurances (DDD-1734A).

**References:** DDD Provider Policy Manual Ch. 52; QVA Service Specifications — Habilitation,
