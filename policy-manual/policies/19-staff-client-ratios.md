## 1. Purpose

To ensure staffing is always adequate and consistent with each member's needs and service
specifications, per the QVA Service Requirements and Service Specifications.

## 2. Policy

FFI's staffing addresses all of the following:

1. **Adequate staffing consistent with member needs** — ratios are set from each member's Planning
   Document and the collective needs of members served together.
2. **Planned absenteeism** — approved leave is backfilled at the time it is approved.
3. **Unexpected absenteeism** — the On-Call Supervisor maintains a same-day replacement roster;
   shifts are never left uncovered where the plan requires support.
4. **Emergencies** — emergency staffing follows FFI-26; the site is staffed as appropriate for the
   immediate circumstance.
5. **Community activities** — ratios for outings are planned in advance based on member needs,
   environment, and risk assessments.
6. **Maintaining required levels** — schedules are built to required ratios; supervisors verify
   coverage daily and adjust immediately when attendance changes.
7. **Ratio maintenance procedure** — service-specific ratio limits are enforced by the scheduling
   system and supervisor review (e.g., ISE job coaching is never less than 1 job coach : 1 member;
   GSE groups are 2–4 members with a paid FFI staff member remaining at the job site at all times;
   PTE does not exceed 1 DSP : 4 members with intermittent 1:1 as needed).
8. **Enhanced ratios** — when a member needs a richer ratio than the standard authorization, the
   manager documents the need and requests Division approval.
9. **Prior Division approval** — enhanced ratios are implemented **only after** approval by the
   Division.
10. **Unsupervised time** — a member's use of unsupervised time follows the member's Planning
    Document. Unsupervised time is never used to meet staffing needs or for staff/vendor
    convenience.
11. **Training before serving** — all staff are trained and oriented to members' specialized needs
    before working with them (FFI-09).
12. **Member choice** — members' right to personal care provided by staff of their choosing,
    including gender preference, is honored in scheduling and matching.

## 3. Monitoring, compliance, and corrective action

Supervisors verify ratios daily; the Quality & Compliance Coordinator audits schedules against
authorizations and plans monthly. Understaffed shifts are service gaps reported per FFI-06 §5 and
corrected; repeated scheduling failures result in CAPs and discipline.

## 4. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI schedule/roster; enhanced ratio request; coverage exception log.

**References:** QVA Service Requirements/Scope of Work & Service Specifications (per-service ratio
rules); FFI-20 (Daily Staffing Schedule for HID settings).
