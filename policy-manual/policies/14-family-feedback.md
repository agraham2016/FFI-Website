## 1. Purpose

To gather, analyze, and act on member and family feedback about FFI services, per the QVA Service
Requirements.

## 2. Policy

- FFI conducts a member/family satisfaction survey **at least annually** for every member served
  (paper, electronic, or verbal administration to match accessibility needs and language, FFI-13).
- Survey topics include: quality and reliability of services, staff professionalism, communication,
  progress toward goals, cultural respect, safety, and overall satisfaction, with space for open
  comments.

## 3. Procedures

1. The Quality & Compliance Coordinator administers the survey each year and compiles results.
2. **Tracking and trending:** results are scored, logged, and compared year-over-year and against
   complaint/incident data.
3. **Review:** the Executive Director and the Program Manager / Employment Services Manager review
   results with the Quality & Compliance Coordinator within 30 days of survey close.
4. **Feedback to stakeholders:** the Executive Director provides formal feedback (a written summary
   of results and planned improvements) to members, families, and staff annually; supervisors
   provide informal feedback in planning meetings and day-to-day contact.
5. **Measuring improvement:** each identified improvement gets a measurable indicator (for
   example, fewer communication complaints, higher on-time service rate, improved satisfaction
   score) tracked in the QM program (FFI-06) and reported in the next annual summary.

## 4. Compliance and corrective action

Low or declining results in any area trigger a documented improvement plan with an owner and due
date. Failure to administer the annual survey is a QM finding corrected by immediate scheduling.

## 5. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI Satisfaction Survey; annual results summary template.

**References:** QVA Service Requirements; FFI-06 Quality Management Plan.
