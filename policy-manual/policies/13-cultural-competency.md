## 1. Purpose

To ensure every member receives services that respect their language, culture, religion, and
identity, per the QVA and DDD Provider Policy Manual Chapter 26.

## 2. Policy and methods

1. **Preferred language.** FFI identifies each member's preferred language at intake and ensures
   services are delivered in it — primarily by recruiting and matching staff who can meet the
   language needs of the members served, and by using qualified interpretation when a match is not
   available.
2. **Interpretation services.** FFI provides qualified interpretation (including telephonic/video
   interpretation services and ASL interpreters) at **no cost to DDD members**.
3. **Written translation.** FFI translates member-facing written documents (plans, schedules,
   progress reports, notices) into the member's/responsible person's language at **no cost**,
   using qualified translation services.
4. **Staff training.** Cultural competency training is provided at hire and annually (Training
   Coordinator; in person or online), covering culturally responsive communication, disability
   culture, religious and dietary accommodations, and LEP obligations. Additional/ongoing training
   and coaching are provided when a member's specific cultural needs call for it (for example,
   before starting services with a member whose traditions staff are unfamiliar with).
5. **Community cultural participation.** FFI encourages and supports member participation in
   cultural events and communities of their choosing — supporting transportation planning, schedule
   accommodations, and skill-building goals tied to cultural life.
6. **Religious and spiritual needs.** FFI supports members' religious practice (attendance,
   dietary rules, observances) and never imposes staff beliefs on members.
7. **Gender identity.** FFI supports each member's gender identity and expression, including using
   the member's preferred name and gender pronouns, and honoring stated gender preferences for
   personal care staffing (FFI-19).
8. **Feedback.** FFI obtains feedback on cultural and individual preferences through the annual
   satisfaction survey (FFI-14), planning meetings, and the complaint process, and adjusts
   services accordingly.

## 3. Monitoring, compliance, and corrective action

The Executive Director owns the plan; the Quality & Compliance Coordinator reviews cultural
accommodation requests, survey results, and complaints annually and drives improvements.
Disrespect of a member's culture, language, religion, or identity results in retraining and
progressive discipline.

## 4. Forms and references

**Forms:** Intake language/cultural preferences form; interpreter request log.

**References:** QVA Service Requirements & Standard Terms and Conditions; DDD Provider Policy
Manual Ch. 26; Title VI of the Civil Rights Act (LEP); ADA.
