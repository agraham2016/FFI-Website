## 1. Purpose

To establish consistent recordkeeping, retention, data security, and time verification standards
across all FFI settings, per the QVA (Standard Terms and Conditions 6.3.1 — current, up-to-date
employee and member records at all times), DDD Provider Policy Manual Chapter 35 (Progress
Reporting), and A.A.C. R6-6-809.N.

## 2. Documentation requirements

- **Employee hours:** all employee hours are tracked and verified. DSP time entries identify the
  member served, service code, date, start/end times, and location.
- **Verification of hours:** the member or guardian signs off on/verifies hours worked by the
  employee in writing (paper or electronic signature) on a recurring basis; supervisors reconcile
  timesheets against schedules and service notes before payroll and billing.
- **Proof of hours:** timesheets, verification signatures, and payroll records are retained as
  proof of hours worked.
- **Attendance logs:** where applicable (group settings, PTE/GSE program activities), FFI
  maintains a log of each member's attendance including arrival and departure times.
- **Teaching strategies:** habilitation teaching strategies (DDD-1783A format) are developed and
  submitted within 20 business days of initiating service, of a change, or when a new outcome is
  identified.

## 3. Progress reporting

| Service | Report | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HAI/HID (Supported Living) and other non-group-home habilitation | Quarterly progress report (DDD-1784A format) to DDD, member/representative, and PCP | July 15, October 15, January 15, April 15 |
| PTE, ISE, GSE, ESA | Quarterly progress report to DDD, member/representative, and PCP | July 15, October 15, January 15, April 15 |
| PTE, ISE, GSE, ESA | Semi-annual report to District Program Manager or designee | January 31 and July 31 |

Progress reports are submitted through the Division's secure FTP process; reports are written in
the language of the member/responsible person (interpretation/translation funded by FFI, see
FFI-13).

## 4. Retention

All member records, service documentation, employee records, timesheets, ledgers, incident
reports, and billing records are retained a minimum of **seven (7) years** (longer where a specific
law requires), then destroyed securely per FFI-01 § 3.6.

## 5. Data security

- Electronic systems: unique user accounts, role-based access, strong passwords, encryption at
  rest and in transit, and prompt deactivation on staff separation.
- Paper records: locked storage, sign-out procedure for temporary removal, no records left
  unattended off-site.
- Backups of electronic records are maintained and tested; loss of systems/records is covered by
  the Contingency Plan (FFI-26).

## 6. Publications review

Any report or publication FFI intends for DDD members or applicants for Division-funded services
is provided to DDD for review before distribution.

## 7. Training, monitoring, and oversight

The Training Coordinator trains all staff at hire and annually on documentation standards and
timesheet integrity. The Quality & Compliance Coordinator audits timesheet/service-note
reconciliation monthly, spot-checks member files quarterly, tracks progress report timeliness
against the calendar above, and reports compliance to the Executive Director.

## 8. Compliance and corrective action

Falsified time or service records are treated under FFI-04 (FWA) and result in discipline up to
termination and repayment. Missed report deadlines trigger a documented cause review and CAP.

## 9. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI timesheet with member/guardian verification; attendance log; DDD-1783A Habilitation
Outcomes Strategies; DDD-1784A Habilitation Progress Report; ISE/GSE/ESA/PTE quarterly and
six-month report formats.

**References:** QVA Standard Terms and Conditions & Service Requirements (Recordkeeping sections of
all Service Specifications); DDD Provider Policy Manual Ch. 35, 41; A.A.C. R6-6-809.N.
