## 1. Purpose

To ensure every staff member is oriented to each member's specific needs **before** delivering
services, per the QVA, DDD form DD-097 (Pre-Service Orientation), and A.A.C. R6-6-808.E & F,
R6-6-1512.C, and R6-6-1527.

## 2. Policy

- FFI uses the Division's Pre-Service Orientation form (**DDD-0097A**, and DDD-0097A-S where
  applicable). If an FFI form is ever substituted, it will contain at least the same information.
- Pre-service orientation is completed, signed, and filed **before** a staff member works with a
  DDD member, and prior authorization for the service is confirmed before service begins.

## 3. Procedures

1. **Completion before service.** The Program Manager or Employment Services Manager schedules the
   orientation as part of assignment; the schedule system blocks a DSP from being scheduled with a
   member until the signed orientation is on file.
2. **Content.** Orientation reviews the member's Planning Document and individualized health and
   safety needs, including behavior plans, medications, allergies, seizure protocols, adaptive
   equipment, communication preferences, emergency contacts, and back-up contacts.
3. **Signatures.** The orientation record is signed and dated by the staff member being oriented
   and by the supervisor or designee verifying the orientation is complete.
4. **Access for review.** Completed orientation documents are kept in the member's file and the
   staff training file; employees may review member-specific orientation information at any time
   through their supervisor (with confidentiality safeguards per FFI-01).
5. **Submission.** Completed and updated orientation documentation is sent to the member's DDD
   Support Coordinator, and updated whenever the member's needs materially change.
6. **Matching and mutual agreement.** FFI matches DSPs to members based on member needs and
   preferences (skills, language, schedule, personality, gender preference for personal care) and
   confirms mutual agreement to serve — the member/responsible person and the DSP each confirm the
   match; either may request a change at any time.

## 4. Training, monitoring, and oversight

The Training Coordinator trains supervisors on conducting orientations. The Quality & Compliance
Coordinator audits new assignments monthly to verify orientation preceded service and that
documentation is complete and filed.

## 5. Compliance and corrective action

A DSP found serving a member without completed orientation is removed from the assignment until
oriented; the scheduling failure is reviewed and corrected. Repeat violations result in
progressive discipline.

## 6. Forms and references

**Forms:** DDD-0097A; DDD-0097A-S.

**References:** QVA Service Requirements/Scope of Work; DDD Provider Policy Manual Ch. 2;
A.A.C. R6-6-808.E & F, R6-6-1512.C, R6-6-1527.
