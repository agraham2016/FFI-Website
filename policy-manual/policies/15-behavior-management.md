## 1. Purpose

To ensure behavior support is positive, person-centered, and compliant with Article 9
(A.A.C. R6-6-900 et seq.), the DDD Behavior Supports Manual (including Chapter 900 — Emergency
Measures and Physical Management Techniques), Provider Policy Manual Chapter 66, and
A.A.C. R6-6-1007, R6-6-1107, R6-6-808.G & I, R6-6-809.B.

## 2. Policy

- **Prevention first.** Staff are trained in prevention and positive behavior support as
  applicable to their assignments: recognizing cues, reinforcing appropriate behavior,
  redirection, environmental evaluation, consistency, clear communication, and offering choices.
- **Article 9 alignment.** All behavior-related practices, plans, and training follow Article 9
  requirements; techniques prohibited by Article 9 and FFI-03 §3 are never used.
- **Emergency measures.** Any use of emergency measures is an incident: staff follow Behavior
  Supports Manual Chapter 900, ensure member safety, and report per FFI-02 (immediate verbal
  report; written report within 24 hours).

## 3. Behavior Treatment Plans (BTPs)

1. **Timely PRC submission.** When a member needs a BTP that requires review (techniques requiring
   force, response cost, potential rights infringement, behavior-modifying medications, or
   protective devices), FFI participates with the planning team so the plan is submitted to the
   Program Review Committee (PRC) — and IOC where applicable — **within 90 days of placement or of
   the need being identified**. The Program Manager tracks submission dates.
2. **Implementation.** Assigned staff are trained on each member's BTP before implementing it;
   implementation is documented every shift.
3. **Effectiveness monitoring.** Behavior data is collected as specified in the BTP, graphed or
   summarized monthly, and reviewed by the Program Manager with the planning team; ineffective
   plans are returned to the team for revision.

## 4. Behavior-modifying medications

For members using behavior-modifying medications, FFI's procedures include:

- **Quarterly medication reviews** documented with the components listed under
  A.A.C. R6-6-806.M (medication, dosage, purpose, effectiveness, side effects, and review
  outcome), coordinated with the prescriber and planning team;
- **Screening for Tardive Dyskinesia** on the schedule set by the prescriber/planning team, with
  results documented; and
- **Prohibitions on psychotropic medication misuse** — never used as punishment, for staff
  convenience, or as a substitute for services, and only with informed consent and PRC review as
  required.

## 5. Documentation of maladaptive behavior

Staff document maladaptive behavior objectively (antecedent–behavior–consequence, duration,
intensity, intervention used, outcome) in the member record the same shift; patterns are raised to
the planning team.

## 6. Training and verification

- DSPs complete positive behavior support training at hire and annually; Prevention & Support
  (certified instructor) where required by a member's plan (FFI-08).
- Direct Care Workers' training on this policy's requirements is documented with signed, dated
  records.
- This policy is reviewed by all DCWs at hire, at least annually, and whenever revised, with
  signed verification tracked by the Training Coordinator.

## 7. Monitoring, compliance, and corrective action

The Program Manager reviews behavior documentation monthly; the Quality & Compliance Coordinator
audits BTP submission timeliness, quarterly medication review documentation, and training
verification quarterly. Use of a prohibited technique triggers immediate removal from member
contact, incident reporting, investigation, and discipline up to termination.
