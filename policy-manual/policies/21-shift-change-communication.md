## 1. Purpose

To ensure member needs and status changes are reliably communicated between staff at shift
changes, per A.A.C. R6-6-803.A, R6-6-806.L & M, R6-6-808.G & I, R6-6-809.G, and R6-6-906.B.

## 2. Policy and procedures

1. **Shift handoff.** Outgoing staff communicate to relieving staff, verbally and in the written
   shift log, any needs and/or changes for each member during the shift: health status, behavior
   events, medications given or due, appointments, mood, meals, and follow-ups needed.
2. **Communication system.** FFI uses a standing site communication log (bound or electronic)
   covering events and changes affecting members' lives; the system is defined across FFI's QM
   plan (FFI-06), policies, training curriculum, and forms.
3. **Monitoring standards.** FFI applies the residential monitoring standards applicable to its
   settings (Article 8-equivalent oversight for supported-living sites): supervisor site visits,
   log reviews, and documentation checks.
4. **Staff questions encouraged.** Relieving staff are empowered and expected to ask questions
   about changes in a member's status before the outgoing staff leaves; leaving before completing
   handoff is a policy violation.
5. **Significant events.** Significant events are reported internally (supervisor/On-Call) and
   externally (Division, guardian) in a timely manner per FFI-02 — including incidents, medication
   errors, and medication changes.
6. **Other providers.** The Program Manager coordinates with members' other service providers (day
   programs, employment services, behavioral health) so services collectively address the member's
   needs; exchanges follow FFI-01 consent requirements.
7. **Documentation.** Internal communication about members is documented in the site
   communication log and member records: date, time, staff, topic, and any action needed.

## 3. Training, monitoring, compliance

Handoff procedures are part of onboarding and site orientation. Supervisors review shift logs
weekly; missed handoffs or undocumented changes result in coaching, retraining, and progressive
discipline.

**Forms:** FFI shift communication log; handoff checklist.
