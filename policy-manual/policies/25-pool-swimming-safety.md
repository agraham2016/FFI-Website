## 1. Purpose

To keep members safe around water, per A.A.C. R6-6-806.S, R6-6-1009.A-1, R6-6-1011.L,
R6-6-1109.A-1, R6-6-1111.L, R6-6-1505.A, and OLCR Life Safety Inspections (Article 7).

## 2. Policy

1. **Bodies of water covered.** This policy applies to all situations involving bodies of water as
   defined by A.A.C. Title 6, Chapter 18, Article 1 — including pools, splash pads, rivers, lakes,
   canals, fountains, hot tubs, and fish ponds — whether at a member's home, in the community, or
   on outings.
2. **Risk assessment first.** Before any activity near water, staff confirm the member's Planning
   Document permissions, swimming ability, seizure risk, and required supervision level.
3. **Certified/licensed settings.** For all settings that are certified or licensed:
   - For any licensed residential setting, pools are enclosed per ADHS rules (A.A.C. Title 9,
     Chapter 33) — self-closing, self-latching barriers meeting code;
   - **Prior consent** is obtained from the member/responsible person indicating limitations of
     use of, or proximity to, bodies of water by the member; and
   - Staff responsible for supervision during water activities are **trained in water safety,
     first aid, and CPR** (current certifications on file).
4. **Supervision.** Members are supervised around water per their plans; staff maintain
   line-of-sight supervision for members with water-risk indicators, and no member is left
   unattended near water where their plan requires supervision.

## 3. Training, monitoring, compliance

Water safety expectations are part of onboarding and site orientation; CPR/First Aid currency is
tracked under FFI-08. Supervisors verify water-related consents before outings involving water.
Violations (unauthorized water activity, lapsed supervision) are treated as serious safety
violations with incident reporting and discipline.

## 4. Forms and references

**Forms:** Water activity consent (LCR-1036A HBPNA basis); outing plan/risk check.

**References:** A.A.C. R6-6-806.S, R6-6-1009.A-1, R6-6-1011.L, R6-6-1109.A-1, R6-6-1111.L,

Inspections — Article 7.
