## 1. Purpose

To define FFI's Quality Management (QM) program: how incidents, complaints, member/family input,
service monitoring, and improvement activities are managed and used to continuously improve
services, per the QVA Service Requirements, Provider Policy Manual Chapters 32 and 42, Behavior
Supports Manual Chapter 900, and A.A.C. R6-6-901.

## 2. Incident management (summary — see FFI-02)

- Incidents are reported internally (staff → On-Call Supervisor → management) and externally
  (Division, APS/DCS/law enforcement as applicable) within the timeframes in FFI-02.
- Family/guardian, Division, and (as applicable) police/APS/DCS notifications follow FFI-02
  timelines (same day for serious incidents; next business day otherwise).
- Reporting protocols are shared with members, families, and guardians at intake and annually.
- Every incident receives an internal review; corrective actions are assigned, implemented, and
  monitored to completion by the Quality & Compliance Coordinator.
- Incidents are logged and coded for tracking and trending; trends are reviewed quarterly.

## 3. Complaints and grievances

- **Who can file:** any member, family member, guardian/representative, staff member, or other
  stakeholder, verbally or in writing, including anonymously.
- **Who resolves:** the Program Manager or Employment Services Manager resolves service-level
  complaints; the Executive Director resolves complaints about managers or the agency.
- **Timeframe:** acknowledgment within 2 business days; resolution or written status within 10
  business days; complex matters no later than 30 days.
- **Monitoring:** the Quality & Compliance Coordinator logs all complaints/grievances, monitors
  resolution timeliness, and trends them quarterly alongside incidents.

## 4. Member, family, and representative input

- Input is actively solicited through: the annual Family Feedback/Satisfaction Survey (FFI-14),
  planning meetings, and an open-door channel to management.
- Satisfaction is measured through the annual survey plus complaint/compliment tracking.
- Members/families are invited to participate in the evaluation of direct service staff (feedback
  is included in staff performance reviews) and in the evaluation and improvement of services.
- FFI commits to at least these activities for active involvement in operations:
  1. **Periodic member/family meetings and open houses** to share agency updates and solicit
     feedback on policies, procedures, and program enhancements; and
  2. **Sharing improvement activity results** (survey results, QM outcomes) with members, families,
     and representatives at least annually.
- Members and families are encouraged to self-direct and manage their services to the extent they
  wish, consistent with person-centered planning.

## 5. Monitoring and evaluation of services

- **Service gap analysis:** the Quality & Compliance Coordinator compares authorized versus
  delivered hours monthly, identifies gaps, reports service gaps to the Division, and drives
  staffing fixes (FFI-19/FFI-20).
- **Planning Document alignment:** supervisors review service notes against each member's
  Planning Document objectives monthly; progress reports (FFI-05) formalize this quarterly.
- **Member well-being:** additional systematic monitoring includes supervisor site visits,
  observation of DSP-member interactions, and health status review (FFI-16).
- **Training quality:** training effectiveness is evaluated through post-training assessments,
  in-class feedback, and supervisor drop-in observations of practice.
- **Financial integrity:** billing accuracy and timeliness are ensured through the controls in
  FFI-04 and FFI-05.
- **Plan governance:** this QM Plan is developed by the Executive Director with the Quality &
  Compliance Coordinator, shared with all staff and with families on request, and reviewed and
  updated at least annually.
- **Physical environments:** FFI-operated or leased environments are evaluated quarterly for
  cleanliness, safety, and risk factors using a site checklist; issues are corrected on a tracked
  CAP.

## 6. Electronic monitoring/surveillance (employment, day program, and residential settings)

If FFI installs or uses any electronic monitoring or surveillance equipment:

1. FFI notifies the District Network Manager **before** installation or use and provides
   policies/procedures/notices demonstrating no member rights under A.R.S. § 36-551.01 are
   violated.
2. In residential settings, surveillance is used only where residing members and their legal
   representatives request or consent to it.
3. Surveillance in common public settings (e.g., workshops, employment programs) is permitted.
4. A sign is posted conspicuously in each common area under surveillance, indicating days and
   hours of surveillance.
5. Surveillance never extends to members' private spaces (bathrooms, bedrooms).
6. Surveillance records are retained per A.R.S. § 12-2297 and produced on request to the member or
   responsible person, the Division, law enforcement, protective agencies, and others entitled to
   access.

## 7. Emergency safeguards and improvement projects

Safeguards protecting members in natural disasters or public emergencies are defined in FFI-26.
When FFI defines annual Quality Improvement projects, each has a written aim, measures, and owner;
results are analyzed against the aim and shared with staff, members, and families.

## 8. Oversight, compliance, and corrective action

The Executive Director holds overall QM accountability; the Quality & Compliance Coordinator
operates the tracking systems and quarterly QM review. Failures to follow QM processes result in
retraining and progressive discipline; systemic issues result in documented CAPs tracked to
completion.

## 9. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI complaint/grievance log; incident tracking log; site safety checklist; annual
survey (FFI-14); CAP template.

**References:** QVA Service Requirements/Scope of Work; Provider Policy Manual Ch. 32, 42;
Behavior Supports Manual Ch. 900; A.A.C. R6-6-901; A.R.S. § 36-551.01, § 12-2297.
