## 1. Purpose

To build and retain a qualified, stable direct support workforce, per DDD Provider Policy Manual
Chapter 63, DDD Operations Policy Manual Chapter 407, and AHCCCS ACOM 407.

## 2. Policy

- FFI develops and maintains a written **Workforce Development Plan** (WDP).
- The WDP contains the required components, including measurable goals for recruitment,
  retention, training, and career development of direct support staff.
- The WDP is **reviewed and updated at least annually**, including a written assessment of
  progress toward each goal.
- Workforce development activities are monitored throughout the year, not just at the annual
  review.
- FFI collects and analyzes workforce data — recruitment results, retention, turnover, and
  time-to-hire — quarterly, plus ad hoc data as needed to answer specific workforce questions.
- WDPs and their annual assessments are maintained on file and submitted to the Division upon
  request.

## 3. Plan components

FFI's WDP addresses, at minimum:

1. **Recruitment** — sourcing channels, hiring standards (FFI-08), and time-to-hire targets.
2. **Retention** — compensation review cadence, recognition, supervisor support, and stay/exit
   interviews.
3. **Training and career development** — onboarding and annual training calendar (FFI-08),
   competency pathways for DSPs (e.g., lead DSP, employment specialist), and tuition/certification
   support where feasible.
4. **Goals and measures** — each goal has a numeric target, data source, owner, and review date.

## 4. Monitoring and oversight

The Executive Director owns the WDP; the Training Coordinator maintains training-related data and
the Quality & Compliance Coordinator compiles quarterly workforce metrics (turnover %, average
time-to-hire, vacancy rate, training compliance %) for leadership review.

## 5. Compliance and corrective action

If quarterly data shows a goal off-track, the Executive Director documents an adjustment plan.
Failure to maintain or produce the WDP is a compliance finding handled with an immediate CAP.

## 6. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI Workforce Development Plan template; quarterly workforce metrics dashboard.

**References:** DDD Provider Policy Manual Ch. 63; DDD Operations Policy Manual Ch. 407; AHCCCS
ACOM 407.
