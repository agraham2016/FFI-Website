## 1. Purpose

To define FFI's role in the person-centered planning process and how FFI implements each member's
Planning Document, per A.A.C. R6-6-805.C, E, F, G, I & J, R6-6-808.G & I, and R6-6-809.M.

## 2. FFI's role in the planning process

Responsibility by title:

- **Program Manager / Employment Services Manager:** reviews each member's Planning Document on
  receipt and before every planning meeting; requests changes when goals, supports, or risk
  information need updating; represents FFI at planning meetings; completes Planning Document
  assignments and team agreements accepted by FFI.
- **Quality & Compliance Coordinator:** ensures Progress Reports are completed and sent on the
  FFI-05 schedule to DDD, the member/representative, and the PCP.
- **DSPs:** implement the plan daily and raise concerns or observed changes to their supervisor.

## 3. Carrying out objectives and assignments

- Each member's goals are translated into teaching strategies (DDD-1783A format) within 20
  business days of service initiation or goal change.
- **Documentation:** DSPs document each shift against plan objectives (services delivered, member
  response, data for each outcome).
- **Barriers to implementation:** when a barrier prevents carrying out an objective (member
  declines, health change, staffing, environmental issue), the DSP documents it the same day; the
  supervisor addresses it within 5 business days and, if unresolved, raises it to the member's
  planning team and Support Coordinator.

## 4. Required plan reviews

Before serving a member, and at every plan update, assigned staff review at minimum:

- the member's **Risk Assessment**, and
- the member's **Back-Up Plan** (emergency contacts and what to do when scheduled supports are
  unavailable),

along with behavior plans, health protocols, and communication preferences (see FFI-09).

## 5. Training, competency, and compliance systems

1. This policy and each member's Planning Document are reviewed with all direct care staff at
   least annually and whenever revised, with signed verification.
2. Comprehension and demonstrated competency are verified: supervisors observe each DSP
   implementing plan strategies within the first 30 days of assignment and at least annually.
3. Employee compliance with, and implementation of, the Planning Document is monitored through
   monthly documentation reviews and supervisor observation; findings feed FFI-06.

## 6. Compliance and corrective action

Failure to implement a member's plan (missed objectives, undocumented services, ignored barriers)
results in retraining and progressive discipline; patterns trigger system CAPs.

## 7. Forms and references

**Forms:** DDD-1783A; DDD-1784A; FFI shift documentation; barrier log.

**References:** A.A.C. R6-6-805.C, E, F, G, I & J; R6-6-808.G & I; R6-6-809.M; DDD Operations
Policy Manual Ch. 2000 (Planning Documents).
