## 1. Purpose

To define the qualifications, background screening, and training every FFI staff member must meet
before and while working with members, per the QVA, DDD Provider Policy Manual Chapters 20, 26,
49, and 61, A.A.C. R6-6-808, R6-6-809.C, R6-6-905, R6-6-906, and A.R.S. §§ 36-558.01 and
36-593.01.

## 2. Hiring requirements

Before working with members, every direct service staff member must have on file:

1. **Valid Class 1 Fingerprint Clearance Card** (verified via the AZ DPS website; front and back
   copied; validity re-verified at renewal).
2. **Minimum age of 18 years.**
3. **Notarized attestation** that the person is not: (i) subject to registration as a sex offender
   in Arizona or any other jurisdiction; (ii) awaiting trial on, or convicted of, committing or
   attempting, soliciting, facilitating, or conspiring to commit any criminal offense listed in
   A.R.S. § 41-1758.03(B); or (iii) any similar offense in another state or jurisdiction.
4. **Notarized Criminal History Self-Disclosure Declaration Statement (LCR-1034A)** — renewed at
   least every 3 years; if signed annually, it may serve in place of the attestation above.
5. **Direct Service Position Declaration Form (DDD-1727A).**
6. **Three references**, documented and available for DDD verification.
7. **CPR certification** — renewed every 2 years (in-person skills demonstration).
8. **First Aid training/certification** — renewed every 2 years.
9. **Monthly SAM and OIG/LEIE exclusion checks** (ongoing, all staff).
10. **Central Registry background checks** (DCS and APS Registry).
11. **Article 9 review** with written documentation — renewed every 3 years, taught by a certified
    instructor.
12. **Any additional training required by an assigned member's Planning Document** before serving
    that member.
13. **Prevention and Support (PS/Article 9 physical management)** certification where required by a
    member's plan.
14. **Verification of date of hire, AZ driver's license (if driving), I-9 / E-Verify lawful
    presence, and previous experience.**

## 3. Training plan

### 3.1 Core onboarding (before member contact)

Orientation to FFI mission and values; member rights (Article 9); HIPAA/confidentiality (FFI-01);
incident reporting and mandatory reporting (FFI-02, FFI-03); FWA (FFI-04); person-centered
practice and the PCSP process (FFI-11); cultural competency (FFI-13); health and medical response
(FFI-16); documentation standards (FFI-05); pre-service orientation to each assigned member
(FFI-09).

### 3.2 Service-specific training

- **Supported Living (HAI/HID):** habilitation outcomes and teaching strategies (DDD-1783A),
  daily-living skill building, community integration, and any member-specific health/behavior
  protocols.
- **Employment services (GSE/ISE/ESA/PTE):** job coaching methods, employer engagement, natural
  supports, benefits basics, and worksite safety (including Arizona OSHA awareness).
- **Transportation (TR1):** responsible driving (Provider Manual Ch. 49) and FFI-27 requirements.

### 3.3 Tracking system

The Training Coordinator maintains a training and qualification matrix for every employee (dates
completed, expiration dates for CPR/First Aid/FPC/Article 9, member-specific trainings) with
automated expiry reminders at 60/30/7 days. FFI's license/training tracking system is available to
the Division on request.

## 4. Key requirements (verification and enforcement)

1. Staff violations of policy are covered in this manual's compliance sections and FFI's
   disciplinary procedure.
2. Every policy in this manual identifies its supporting training.
3. Training frequency is defined per topic (onboarding, annual refresher, 2-year CPR/First Aid,
   3-year Article 9/LCR-1034A).
4. Training method is identified per topic (in-person for skills like CPR and Prevention &
   Support; in-person or online for knowledge topics).
5. Comprehension and competency are verified by quiz, skills demonstration, or supervisor
   observation, and documented.
6. Compliance monitoring: the Quality & Compliance Coordinator audits personnel files quarterly
   against §2 and the training matrix.
7. Violations result in immediate removal from schedules until cured, plus progressive discipline
   and systemic CAPs.
8. Oversight responsibility: Executive Director (policy), Training Coordinator (delivery/records),
   Quality & Compliance Coordinator (auditing).
