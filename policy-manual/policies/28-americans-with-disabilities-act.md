## 1. Purpose

To ensure members and their Responsible Persons can fully access FFI's services, programs, and
communications, and can request reasonable modifications when a disability makes that necessary,
per Title II of the ADA (28 CFR Part 35), A.R.S. § 41-1492.06(B), A.R.S. § 11-1024, and DDD
Provider Policy Manual Chapter 24 (Americans with Disabilities Act).

## 2. Policy

- FFI does not discriminate on the basis of disability and makes reasonable modifications to its
  policies, practices, and procedures when needed to avoid discrimination, unless the modification
  would fundamentally alter the nature of the service or create an undue administrative or
  financial burden.
- FFI provides auxiliary aids and services, at no cost to the member, when needed for effective
  communication with members and Responsible Persons who have communication disabilities.
- The Executive Director is FFI's ADA Coordinator and is responsible for receiving, evaluating,
  deciding, and documenting ADA requests.

## 3. Notifying Responsible Persons how to request a modification

At intake, at each annual planning meeting, and whenever asked, FFI gives the member and
Responsible Person a written notice (in their preferred language and format) explaining:

1. their right to request a reasonable modification or an auxiliary aid or service;
2. how to make a request: verbally or in writing to any FFI staff member, or directly to the ADA
   Coordinator at (602) 663-1664 or foundationforindependence@gmail.com;
3. how FFI evaluates requests and the timeframe for a decision; and
4. how to ask the Division for help, including the DDD ADA Requests Unit
   (DDDADArequests@azdes.gov).

The notice is also posted at FFI's office and at each supported-living site, and a copy of the
signed acknowledgment is kept in the member record.

## 4. Evaluating requests

1. **Receipt.** Any staff member who receives a request (verbal or written) records it the same
   day on the FFI ADA Request Log and forwards it to the ADA Coordinator.
2. **Interactive discussion.** Within 5 business days, the ADA Coordinator contacts the member or
   Responsible Person to understand the barrier, the modification requested, and possible
   alternatives. Documentation of the disability is requested only when the need is not obvious,
   and only as much as is necessary.
3. **Evaluation.** The ADA Coordinator considers whether the modification is needed because of the
   member's disability, whether it is effective, and whether it would fundamentally alter the
   service or create an undue administrative or financial burden. When more than one effective
   option exists, FFI considers the member's preference first.
4. **Timeframe.** A decision is made within 15 business days of receipt; urgent health or safety
   requests are decided as quickly as possible, and interim measures are put in place while a
   request is pending.

## 5. Written determinations and records

- The ADA Coordinator sends the member and Responsible Person a written determination stating
  whether the request is granted, granted with an alternative, or elevated to the Division, the
  reasons for the decision, how the modification will be carried out, and how to ask for
  reconsideration or contact the DDD ADA Requests Unit.
- The request, notes from the interactive discussion, any supporting documents, and the written
  determination are kept in the member record and in FFI's ADA file for at least seven years
  (FFI-05). The ADA Request Log tracks each request from receipt to resolution.

## 6. Auxiliary aids and services

FFI informs members and families, in the notice in §3 and during orientation, that auxiliary aids
and services are available when needed for effective communication, such as qualified sign
language interpreters, assistive listening devices, large print, Braille, audio or electronic
formats, plain-language materials, picture- or symbol-based communication supports, and extra
time for communication. Interpretation and translation are coordinated with FFI-13.

## 7. Accessibility features

FFI informs members, at intake and in its member information materials, about the accessibility
features it offers, including accessible locations for meetings, transportation planned around
the member's mobility needs (FFI-27), documents available in alternative formats, and
communication by phone, text, email, or video according to the member's needs.

## 8. Elevating requests to the Division's Title II ADA Unit

When the ADA Coordinator determines that a requested modification would fundamentally alter the
nature of the program, service, or activity, or would create an undue administrative or financial
burden, FFI does not deny the request on its own. Within 2 business days of that determination,
the ADA Coordinator elevates the request to the Division's Title II ADA Unit through the DDD ADA
Requests Unit (DDDADArequests@azdes.gov), notifies the member's Support Coordinator, and informs
the member and Responsible Person in writing that the request has been elevated. FFI follows the
Division's determination and keeps it with the request record.

## 9. Training

All staff are trained on the ADA at hire and annually by the Training Coordinator (in person or
online), covering members' rights under the ADA, how to recognize and record a modification
request, auxiliary aids and services, respectful disability etiquette, and this policy's
procedures. Completion and comprehension are documented in the staff training file (FFI-08).

## 10. Monitoring, compliance, and corrective action

The Quality & Compliance Coordinator reviews the ADA Request Log quarterly for timeliness and
complete documentation and reports trends to the Executive Director as part of the Quality
Management Plan (FFI-06). Failure to record or forward a request, or to follow this policy,
results in retraining and progressive discipline; systemic issues result in a corrective action
plan.

## 11. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI ADA Rights and Request Notice (with member acknowledgment); FFI ADA Request Log;
FFI ADA Written Determination letter.

**References:** 28 CFR Part 35 (Title II), Nondiscrimination on the Basis of Disability in State
and Local Government Services; A.R.S. § 41-1492.06(B); A.R.S. § 11-1024; DDD Provider Policy
Manual Chapter 24, Americans with Disabilities Act.
