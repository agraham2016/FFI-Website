## 1. Purpose

To set interim standards for members who hold a valid Arizona medical marijuana card, pending
final Division guidance. *Note: the corresponding DDD-2051A checklist (2.20) is under review by
the Division of Developmental Disabilities; FFI will align this policy with Division guidance when
issued.*

## 2. Policy

- FFI recognizes lawful medical marijuana use by adult members holding a valid AMMA (A.R.S.
  Title 36, Chapter 28.1) registry card, as a member right subject to the same plan-based
  parameters as other substances (FFI-23) and to federal law constraints.
- **Staff never purchase, transport, administer, or handle marijuana for members.** Member
  self-administration follows the member's documented plan; storage in shared homes is secured in
  the member's private space, inaccessible to other members.
- Use is documented in the member's planning documents (prescribing/certifying provider, form,
  parameters), reviewed with the planning team, and coordinated with prescribers given medication
  interactions (FFI-15 §4, FFI-17).
- Staff use or possession at any service setting or while on duty is prohibited regardless of
  cardholder status.
- Smoked or vaporized use follows the smoke-free rules in FFI-23 §3.

## 3. Monitoring, compliance, and corrective action

The Program Manager verifies planning-document alignment for any member using medical marijuana;
issues (unsecured storage, staff handling, effects on health/safety) are addressed through the
planning team, incident reporting where applicable, and staff discipline where staff violated this
policy.

## 4. Forms and references

**References:** A.R.S. Title 36, Chapter 28.1 (AMMA); Federal Controlled Substances Act (21
U.S.C. § 811); A.A.C. R9-17-202, R9-17-204, R9-17-310; DDD guidance (pending — under Division
