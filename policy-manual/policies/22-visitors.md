## 1. Purpose

To support members' right to visitors while maintaining safety, per DDD Operations Policy Manual
Chapter 1001-C, Eligibility Policy Manual Chapter 100, A.A.C. R6-6-108.H, R6-6-804, R6-6-1006.J,
R6-6-1009.A, R6-6-1014, R6-6-1114, and A.R.S. § 36-551.01.

## 2. Policy

- **Members' rights.** Members living in supported-living settings may receive visitors of their
  choosing at times of their choosing. FFI does not restrict member visitation **unless** a
  specific, documented health or safety risk is identified in the member's Planning Document — and
  then only to the extent the plan specifies.
- **Staff visitors.** Staff personal visitors are not permitted at member homes or program sites
  while staff are on duty, except with advance supervisor approval for a work-related purpose.
  Staff visitors never assume any care duties and are never left alone with members.
- **Roommates/households.** In shared supported-living homes, visiting arrangements respect all
  members of the household; conflicts are resolved through house meetings and planning teams, not
  blanket bans.

## 3. Procedures

1. **Sign-in/sign-out logs** are maintained at FFI program sites (and at shared supported-living
   sites where the household agrees or a plan requires): visitor name, member visited, time in/out.
2. **Ratios maintained.** Required staffing ratios (FFI-19) are maintained while visitors are on
   site; visitors do not count toward staffing.
3. **Safety.** Staff may ask a visitor to leave when behavior threatens any member's health or
   safety, and document the event (incident report if applicable).

## 4. Training, monitoring, compliance

Visitation rights and procedures are covered in onboarding. Supervisors review visitor logs during
site visits. Unjustified visitor restrictions are rights violations — corrected immediately,
documented, and addressed with retraining/discipline.
