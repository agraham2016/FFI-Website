## 1. Purpose

To safeguard, account for, and (when lost through FFI fault) replace member property and funds,
per the QVA, DDD Operations Policy Manual Chapters 1001-A and 4004, and A.A.C. R6-6-807.A and
R6-6-809.I.

## 2. Policy

- FFI safeguards member property and funds in its care; members' money is theirs, used only for
  their benefit and per their choices.
- FFI accepts responsibility for **acceptance, retention, disposition, accounting for, and
  administration** of member funds it handles under a member's Planning Document.
- **Joint accounts between members and FFI (or staff) are prohibited.** Staff never borrow from,
  lend to, or commingle funds with members.
- Member funds are never loaned, given, or provided in any way to other members, provider staff,
  relatives, or friends.

## 3. Member fund transactions when the Division is payee

Funds received through the Division's Client Funds system are handled through the Qualified Vendor
Administration Site (not directly to staff), and:

- **Excess funds** are not used for non-approved purchases. If disbursed funds exceed the cost of
  the approved purchase, the excess is returned to the member's account with a reconciliation
  statement accounting for purchases.
- All non-personal spending money disbursed from the member's account for goods or services is
  **verified within 30 days by an itemized receipt**.

## 4. Discretionary funds and security

- Parameters for FFI access to and oversight of a member's discretionary spending money are set in
  the Planning Document; staff document all transactions they assist with.
- Member funds held at a site are secured in **locked storage** (locked cash box within a locked
  cabinet), access limited to the supervisor on duty, counted at each shift change involving
  custody transfer.
- Member funds held by FFI do not exceed **$200** per member; amounts above $200 are returned to
  the Client Fund System (or the representative payee) promptly.

## 5. Ledgers and reporting

- A **separate ledger** is maintained for each member's funds featuring: running totals, source of
  funds, date received, all expenditures with purchase descriptions, and attached receipts.
- Ledgers are sent to DDD Client Funds **monthly, on or before the 15th** (late ledgers block
  release of additional client funds until received).
- Ledger records are retained a minimum of **seven years** (FFI-05).

## 6. Property inventory (residential)

FFI maintains an inventory of each supported-living member's significant belongings, updated as
items are accrued or discarded, and sends the updated inventory to the member's representative and
Support Coordinator as changes are made.

## 7. Lost or stolen funds/property

Lost or stolen member funds or property are reported to the member/responsible person and **to the
Division within 10 working days**, and are **replaced by FFI within 10 working days** where loss
resulted from FFI's handling. FFI documents resolution and any repayment.

## 8. Representative payees

When DDD is not the member's representative payee, FFI coordinates with the actual representative
payee (family member, agency, or the member) for receipts, reporting, and reconciliation, and
documents the arrangement in the member record.

## 9. Training, monitoring, compliance

Client funds training is completed before any staff member handles member money, and annually. The
Quality & Compliance Coordinator reconciles ledgers monthly, audits receipts quarterly, and
verifies inventory updates. Violations (missing funds, undocumented transactions, prohibited
transactions) trigger immediate investigation (FFI-02/FFI-04), discipline up to termination,
restitution, and system CAPs.

## 10. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI member funds ledger; property inventory; receipt file; funds count sheet.

**References:** QVA Standard Terms and Conditions & Service Specifications; DDD Operations Policy
Manual Ch. 1001-A, 4004 (A–O); A.A.C. R6-6-807.A, R6-6-809.I.
