## 1. Purpose

To ensure members' routine and emergency health and medical needs are met, per the QVA, DDD
Provider Policy Manual Chapters 5, 7, and 8, A.A.C. R6-6-806 (incl. C & E), R6-6-808.G & I,
R6-6-1011, and A.R.S. § 36-557.Q.

## 2. Routine medical care

- FFI supports members to access routine care (primary care, dental, vision, specialists) as
  identified in their Planning Documents — scheduling support, transportation coordination, and
  accompaniment when authorized.
- **Consent:** routine medical care occurs with the documented consent of the member or
  responsible person; consent status and who may consent are verified at intake and documented in
  the member record.
- **Documentation:** FFI maintains documentation of all medical appointments, emergency room
  visits, lab work, professional consultations, and treatments it supports, in the member record.
- **Supported Living (residential) members:** FFI supports members to see their primary care
  provider for well checks **at least annually** and completes any additional medical follow-up the
  PCP orders. FFI provides results of all healthcare appointments to the guardian **at least
  monthly** (and immediately for significant findings), per the member's release preferences.

## 3. Emergency medical care

- **Non-licensed staff call 911 and perform CPR** until a licensed healthcare professional
  arrives — **even when an Advance Directive is in place**. FFI staff do not interpret or execute
  advance directives in the field.
- After the member is stabilized, staff follow FFI-02: notify the On-Call Supervisor, responsible
  party, Division, and document the event.

## 4. Training

All staff are trained (at hire and annually; Training Coordinator; in-person skills components for
CPR/First Aid) in:

1. Detection of signs of injury and/or illness;
2. Recognizing changes in health status (baseline awareness for assigned members);
3. Infectious disease precautions and the notification process (exposure reporting, standard
   precautions);
4. Response to non-emergency conditions that require medical attention (when to call the PCP,
   nurse line, or responsible person); and
5. Medical emergency procedures and rendering emergency care (911, CPR/First Aid, staying with
   the member).

## 5. Monitoring, compliance, and corrective action

Supervisors review health documentation monthly; the Quality & Compliance Coordinator audits
annual well-check completion and guardian reporting quarterly for supported-living members.
Failures (missed appointments without follow-up, unreported health changes) trigger retraining,
CAPs, and discipline as warranted.

## 6. Forms and references

**Forms:** FFI medical appointment log; health change note; consent for routine care.

**References:** QVA Standard Terms and Conditions; DDD Provider Policy Manual Ch. 5, 7, 8;
A.A.C. R6-6-806, R6-6-806.C & E, R6-6-808.G & I, R6-6-1011; A.R.S. § 36-557.Q.
