## 1. Purpose

To ensure safe, reliable member transportation, per the QVA Service Specifications
(Transportation), DDD Provider Policy Manual Chapters 38 and 49, DDD Medical Policy Manual
Chapters 900 and 1250-H, and A.A.C. R6-6-1006.H, R6-6-1012, R6-6-1112, R6-6-1512.H.

## 2. Section A — operations

1. **Communication:** each vehicle used for member transport is equipped with a two-way radio or a
   cellular phone.
2. **Maintenance records:** vehicle maintenance records and safety inspection files are maintained
   and tracked for every vehicle used to transport members.
3. **Vehicle source:** FFI identifies, per assignment, whether employees drive FFI vehicles or
   their own vehicles. When personal vehicles are used, the same safety, insurance, and
   maintenance-record standards apply.
4. **Driver files:** each driver's license, vehicle registration, and car insurance are kept on
   file and verified at hire and annually.
5. **Member information:** drivers are provided with the health and safety information of all
   members being transported (seizure protocols, behavior considerations, communication needs,
   emergency contacts) before transport (FFI-09).
6. **Wheelchair transport:** members in wheelchairs are transported only in vehicles equipped with
   floor-mounted seat belts, shoulder straps, and wheelchair lock-downs or comparable safety
   equipment; staff are trained on securement before transporting.

## 3. Vehicle requirements

All vehicles used to transport members must have:

- Functional seat belts (all seats fastened to the vehicle body; members properly seated and
  belted);
- Functional wheelchair mounts/lifts (if applicable);
- Age-, size-, and weight-appropriate car seats available when needed to transport children;
- A system for communication in case of emergency (§2.1);
- A end-of-route check system ensuring all personal belongings are removed;
- A end-of-route check system ensuring **all members have exited the vehicle** (walk-through
  check, documented);
- Current valid registration and license plates;
- Operational heating and air conditioning;
- A First Aid kit; and
- The contractually required level of liability insurance (State of AZ Risk Management minimums).

## 4. Maintenance

For all vehicles used to transport DDD members — contracted and/or personal — FFI maintains
records of normal/regular service and maintenance on file (oil changes, brakes, tires,
inspections), reviewed by the Employment Services Manager quarterly.

## 5. TR1-specific requirements

For Transportation (TR1) service, FFI additionally:

1. Maintains a **back-up plan** activated whenever a scheduled driver or vehicle is late by 20
   minutes or more (substitute driver/vehicle dispatch and member/family notification);
2. Keeps **logs of each member's pick-up and drop-off**, including time picked up from home, time
   dropped off at program, time departed program, and time dropped off at home;
3. Maintains on file a **daily transportation log** of services delivered to each member; and
4. Ensures availability of age-appropriate car seats (if applicable).

FFI provides coordinated, reliable transportation so members arrive on time for scheduled
activities and are picked up upon completion.

## 6. Training, monitoring, compliance

Drivers complete responsible driving training (Provider Manual Ch. 49) and vehicle/securement
orientation before transporting members, with annual refreshers. The Employment Services Manager
audits driver files, vehicle checklists, and TR1 logs monthly. Accidents or member injuries during
transport are incidents (FFI-02). Violations (unauthorized drivers, missing restraints, falsified
logs) result in immediate removal from driving duties and progressive discipline.
