# Agency Profile

## Who we are

Foundation for Independence Holdings LLC ("FFI," "the Agency," or "the Qualified Vendor") is an
Arizona provider of person-centered Supported Living and Employment Services for adults and
transition-age young adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities. FFI delivers services
under a Qualified Vendor Agreement with the Arizona Department of Economic Security, Division of
Developmental Disabilities (DES/DDD, "the Division").

Our mission is to help every member we serve build practical life skills, gain meaningful
employment, and live with greater independence in their own community.

## Organizational responsibility

Throughout this manual, responsibility for implementation, training, monitoring, and corrective
action is assigned by title. FFI's leadership roles are:

| Title | Core responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Executive Director | Overall policy ownership, contract compliance, final corrective action authority |
| Program Manager (Supported Living) | Day-to-day implementation and oversight for HAI/HID services |
| Employment Services Manager | Day-to-day implementation and oversight for GSE, ISE, ESA, PTE, and TR1 |
| Quality & Compliance Coordinator | Policy tracking systems, audits, incident trending, quality management |
| Training Coordinator | Staff onboarding, training delivery, training records |
| On-Call Supervisor | After-hours coverage and emergency response at (602) 663-1664 |

In a small-agency configuration, one person may hold more than one title; accountability follows
the title, not the individual.
