# How This Manual Is Organized

This manual was developed using the Division's **Policy Development Tool (DDD-2051A, 07/25)** and is
organized so each FFI policy maps directly to the applicable DDD policy development checklist. The
policy set reflects the services FFI is approved to provide (Supported Living and Employment
Services, with employment-related Transportation); checklists that apply only to services FFI does
not provide (for example, Developmental Homes, Attendant Care/Housekeeping monitoring, and
Electronic Visit Verification) are intentionally not included.

## Standard structure of every policy

In line with DDD-2051A 1.3 (Key Components for Written Policy Content), every policy in this
manual contains:

1. **Document header** — document number, name, effective date, policy owner, and the services it
   applies to.
2. **Purpose and description** — why the policy exists and what it covers.
3. **Policy** — the standards FFI commits to.
4. **Procedures** — how staff carry the policy out, including required forms and timeframes.
5. **Training requirements** — what training supports the policy, who develops and delivers it, and
   how it is documented.
6. **Documentation and recordkeeping** — the records kept, where, and for how long.
7. **Monitoring and oversight** — the tracking systems used, the staff (by title) responsible for
   oversight, and how results feed continuous improvement.
8. **Reporting requirements** — internal and external reporting obligations and timeframes.
9. **Compliance and corrective action** — how infractions are addressed, from retraining through
   disciplinary action, and how systemic issues are corrected.
10. **Forms and references** — associated forms (appendix) and the DDD manuals, Arizona
    Administrative Code (A.A.C.), and Arizona Revised Statutes (A.R.S.) provisions the policy
    implements.

## HCBS Rules commitment

FFI's policies are written to reflect full compliance with the federal Home and Community Based
Services (HCBS) Rules issued by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS). Members FFI
serves have full access to the benefits of community living and receive services in the most
integrated setting appropriate to their needs and preferences. See policy FFI-07 (HCBS Rules
Compliance) for the agency-wide standard.

## Definitions used throughout

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Member | An individual determined eligible for and receiving Division-funded services |
| Responsible person | The member's guardian, designated representative, or (for minors) parent |
| DSP | Direct Support Professional — any FFI direct service staff |
| Planning Document / PCSP | The member's Person-Centered Service Plan and related planning documents |
| PRC | Program Review Committee |
| QVA | Qualified Vendor Agreement |
| Division / DDD | Arizona DES, Division of Developmental Disabilities |

## Review cycle

The full manual is reviewed at least annually by the Executive Director and Quality & Compliance
Coordinator, and upon any change to the QVA, Division policy, or applicable law. Each policy's
"Last Reviewed" date is updated when reviewed, whether or not changes were made.
